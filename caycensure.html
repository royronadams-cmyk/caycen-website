Why it exists

Most insurers your size run four systems and a back office that re-types between them.

CaycenSure replaces them with one. A policy transaction raises its invoice, posts its journal, cedes to the treaty, earns its premium and lands in the return — without anyone touching it twice.

Key features

Everything a general insurer does, in the order it does it.

19 products, configured not coded

Private and commercial motor, fleet, homeowners, commercial property, marine, transit, engineering, liability, PI, fidelity, burglary, money, bonds. Rates, rules and wordings are data your underwriters edit, with maker-checker approval.

Motor done properly for the ECCU

Certificates and schedules that cite your territory's Third-Party Risks Act and its limits; one certificate per vehicle on a fleet; cover notes; named and young drivers; statutory minimums enforced.

SME package policies

One package number over property, liability, burglary, money and more — discount tiers by section or premium, included extensions, one schedule and one invoice — while every section stays a proper policy in the books.

Claims with control

Reserves and payments within each handler's authority, approval queues, salvage and subrogation, adjuster appointments, every letter stored and verifiable; IBNR by rate table, triangle or Bornhuetter–Ferguson.

Billing and accounting built in

Open-item debtors with instalments, receipts, refunds and write-offs; a full double-entry ledger; agency and broker sub-ledgers and remittances; payment runs with ECACH bank files; a period close that books UPR, deferred commission and IBNR.

Reinsurance that administers itself

Quota share, surplus, facultative and excess of loss attached to any products; every transaction ceded automatically, recoveries computed on every reserve movement; treaty statements, bordereaux and settlements.

IFRS 17 and the regulatory return

PAA and GMM measurement, risk adjustment, onerous contracts, reinsurance held and transition — one journal per valuation. The ECCU quarterly return filled into the regulator's own workbook from the books.

Documents people can trust

Signatures on file applied by authority; a QR code and public verification page on every certificate, schedule, receipt and claim letter; PDF and email from any screen.

Underwriting

Quote any product in minutes. Bind it in one.

The risk form is generated from the product's own schema and rated as you type. A quote refers itself when a rule or the underwriter's own authority says so; discounts are picked from a list and checked against authority; taxes and charges follow your tables, passed on or absorbed the way your market does it.

  • Endorsement catalogue tied to the product — a motor endorsement never appears on a property policy
  • Renewals roll forward with depreciation and a no-claim discount read from the claims register
  • Co-insurance, lead or follower, handled at transaction level so your books carry only your share
  • Broker and agency logins scoped to their own book, with statements and commission

Claims

Reserves by method. Payments by authority.

A claim is registered against the policy's real cover trigger — occurrence, claims-made, discovery or project period — with the loss date checked against the period of cover. Each head of damage carries its claimant, excess, reserve and paid; reserves move only with a reason and within authority; payments are approved and paid through the same runs as refunds and commission.

  • Salvage, subrogation, event codes for catastrophes, reopen and close
  • Acknowledgements, reminders, settlement offers, discharge vouchers and repudiations — stored and verifiable
  • Development triangles and accident-year summaries your actuary can read the IBNR rate from

Finance and compliance

Books the regulator, the auditor and the reinsurer can trust.

A real insurance chart of accounts with every posting automatic and every correction a reversal. Statements, ageing, trial balance, commission and producer portfolios export to Excel, CSV and PDF. The period close compares what the books hold with what the reserves require and posts only the difference. IFRS 17 is a measurement layer over the same regulatory books, so both sets of statements come from one source.

  • Fourteen management reports, filterable by product, period and party, with drill-down
  • Data migration toolkit: your book arrives as it stands at cutover, policy numbers kept, with a reconciliation workbook for sign-off
  • Every certificate, schedule, receipt and letter verifiable from the QR code it carries

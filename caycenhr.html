Payroll and HR, done the Saint Lucian way

CAYCEN HR is built on Saint Lucia's statute — cumulative PAYE, NIC, severance, vacation law, and the public-holiday calendar are the engine, not a setting. Every plan, including Essentials, gets flawless statutory compliance: correctness is never a paid upgrade.

Statutory engine

Cumulative PAYE with in-period refund policy, NIC with current caps, IRD-approved allowances, payslips, C3 & C2 schedules, TD4/TD5 — filed-ready, every month.

Employee portal

Staff see their own payslips, request leave with medical certificates attached, maintain their contact and emergency details, and acknowledge company policies — from any phone.

Managers approve

Your organisation chart routes leave requests to the right manager automatically. Decisions are one tap; everything is audited.

Insights & board pack

Headcount, turnover, sick absence, overtime %, vacation and redundancy liability in EC$ — with a one-page printable board pack directors actually read.

One-file payday

A bank-ready payment file covers every salary and every deduction remittance in a single download, generated only from committed, immutable figures.

Bank-grade controls

Two-factor authentication for administrators, separation of duties (no one both prepares and releases money), immutable committed runs, and a full audit trail.

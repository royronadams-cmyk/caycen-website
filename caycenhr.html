Payroll and HR, done the island's way

CaycenHR is built on each island's own statute — the engine, not a setting. Saint Lucia: cumulative PAYE, NIC, the Labour Code's working day, severance and vacation law, the national minimum wage. St Vincent & the Grenadines: PAYE, NIS, the Protection of Employment Act, and the 2024 Wages Regulation Orders for every sector from hotels to call centres. Every plan, including Essentials, gets flawless statutory compliance: correctness is never a paid upgrade.

Statutory engine, both islands

Cumulative PAYE with in-period refund policy, NIC/NIS with current caps, IRD-approved allowances, payslips, C3 & C2 schedules, C5 and the NIS e-Submit file, TD4, consolidated and individual TD5s, the SVG IRD PAYE upload workbook to the IRD's own template — filed-ready every month, year-end on one click. Conversion mid-year carries your opening balances into every report.

Two jobs, one correct withholding

Many islanders work for two employers. Declare the other salary — or, when the other employer is also on CaycenHR, link it with the employee's consent — and this payroll withholds PAYE at the rate the combined income actually falls in and NIS/NIC only up to the shared ceiling. Less to settle on the annual return, nothing over-contributed. No one else does this.

Pension & medical benefits

Two-payer benefits with an employee share and an employer share — fixed or a percentage of pay — shown on the payslip by name, posted to the ledger as a cost and a single payable per provider, remitted on one schedule. Pre-tax and taxable-benefit treatment are the accountant's switches.

Hourly, daily and salaried

Pay by the hour or the day with the rate and the hours shown on the run and the payslip. One unpaid lunch hour, the island's paid day and daily overtime by the Labour Code or the sector's Wages Regulation Order — applied the same way to a typed timesheet, a planned shift or a clocked day.

Employee portal

Staff see their own payslips and year-end TD5, request leave with medical certificates attached, see their published shifts, keep their contact and emergency details current, declare a second job, acknowledge company policies and raise a help-desk ticket — from any phone.

Managers approve

Your organisation chart routes leave requests to the right manager automatically. Pay changes go through an approval chain with evidence attached. Decisions are one tap; everything is audited.

Shifts & time clock

Plan the week, publish it — staff are emailed their shifts and acknowledge them — and print the roster for the wall. A shared tablet at the door with a PIN per person is the clock; managers review late and early minutes and write the week to payroll. Workforce-time KPIs on Insights: punctuality, no-shows, schedule adherence, overtime and labour cost, week by week.

Insights & board pack

Headcount, turnover, sick absence, overtime %, vacation and severance liability in EC$ — with a one-page printable board pack directors actually read, and seventeen range reports from the payroll register, remittances and benefits to attendance, punctuality, labour cost, overtime, conversion balances and the audit exception report.

ECACH payday & the ledger

The ECACH upload file in the ECCB layout — every salary and every credit-union or finance-house remittance in one download, with allocation lists per institution — generated only from committed, immutable figures, against an island-wide institution library. Journals post to Xero or QuickBooks Online, or export as CSV for any ledger. Loans and advances run on a ledger with deferrals.

Bank-grade controls, SME-sized

Two-factor authentication for every money role, separation of duties by default — and when one trusted person runs the whole payroll, the owner grants a standing exception with their authenticator code, every such run visibly stamped. A pre-commit exception screen catches duplicates, negative nets, variances and missing people before anything is released; committed runs are immutable; everything is audited.

Groups and one login

One login, every company you hold a role on — across both islands — with the role you hold on each. Group insights and reports across your entities, a role applied to the whole group in one act, transfers of staff between entities with their service recognised, and owners who are also on their own payroll.

Help inside the app

A help desk routed to your firm first and to Caycen when needed, with service-level clocks by plan, plus branded manuals and a what's-new feed — no separate portal, no lost e-mails.

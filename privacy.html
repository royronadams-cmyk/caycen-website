Who we are and what this covers

CaycenHR is a payroll and human-resources platform operated by Caycen Group, LLC (“Caycen”, “we”) for employers in the Eastern Caribbean. This policy explains what personal data the platform holds, why, where it is kept, who can see it, and the rights people have in respect of it. It covers the platform at hr.caycengroup.com and partner-branded addresses under it, the employee self-service portal, and the connections the platform makes to accounting software on a client's instruction.

In data-protection terms, the client company (the employer) is the controller of its employees' data: it decides what is entered and who in its organisation may see it. Caycen is the processor, acting on the client's instructions under the Client Platform Terms. Where a client is served through a partner accounting firm, the partner acts on the client's behalf under its engagement with the client.

What data the platform holds

Employee records entered by the employer or its partner firm: name, date of birth, contact details and address, emergency contacts, national identifiers required for payroll (tax identification number, national-insurance number), job, department, pay basis and rate, bank account for salary payment, leave and sickness records, loans and salary assignments, documents the employer files (contracts, letters, certificates), and where an employee chooses, a profile photo.

Pay records generated by the platform: pay runs, payslips, statutory deductions, year-end slips and the schedules and returns required by the tax and social-security authorities of the client's jurisdiction.

User accounts for people who sign in: name, e-mail, role, the authenticator used for multi-factor sign-in, and sign-in events.

Audit trail: an append-only record of who did what and when on the platform — every change to an employee record, every approval, every pay-run commit and every document download — kept so that payroll figures can be relied on and reconstructed.

The platform does not use tracking cookies or advertising identifiers. It sets only the session token needed to keep a user signed in.

Why we process it

To compute and record payroll correctly under the law of the client's jurisdiction; to produce payslips, statutory filings and remittances; to let employees see their own pay and leave and request changes; to let authorised users approve and commit pay; to post payroll journals to the client's accounting system when the client connects one; to keep the audit trail that payroll law and good practice require; to keep the platform secure; and to support the client and its partner firm. We process personal data because the client instructs us to under the Client Platform Terms and because the client is legally obliged to keep these records.

Where it is kept

The platform is hosted on DigitalOcean's App Platform in the United States (New York region), with the database and document vault in the same provider. Backups are held with the same provider. Data in transit is encrypted (TLS); credentials, authenticator secrets and accounting-connection tokens are stored encrypted at rest; the document vault is private storage not reachable without a signed-in session.

The governing data-protection law for a client's data is the law of the client's jurisdiction as configured in the platform (Saint Lucia or St Vincent and the Grenadines today), as set out in clause 6 of the Client Platform Terms.

Who can see it

The client's own users, according to the roles the client assigns: an employee sees only their own record; managers see their teams; payroll and administrative roles see what their role requires. Every role is enforced by the platform and recorded in the audit trail.

The client's partner firm, where the client is served through one, for the companies that firm manages and nothing else.

Caycen staff, only to operate the platform and support the client, under confidentiality obligations and on an audited basis.

Service providers we rely on to run the platform: DigitalOcean (hosting, database, storage, in the United States); our transactional e-mail provider (for sign-in, password and notification messages). They process data only on our instructions.

Accounting software, only when a client connects one: if a client links its company to Xero or QuickBooks Online, the platform reads that organisation's chart of accounts and posts payroll journal entries to it. Journal entries contain account totals and the names of institutions paid; they do not contain individual employees' pay. The client can disconnect at any time from the platform or from within the accounting software. Nothing read from an accounting system is used for any other purpose.

Authorities: statutory filings are produced for the client to submit; Caycen does not file on a client's behalf unless separately engaged to.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use personal data or accounting data to train, fine-tune or improve any artificial-intelligence model.

How long we keep it

For as long as the client's company is on the platform, plus the period after termination set out in the Client Platform Terms (clause 12), during which the client can export everything. Committed pay runs, statutory outputs and the audit trail are kept for the retention period payroll and tax law requires, as the client instructs, and are then deleted. Backups roll off on a fixed cycle after deletion.

Your rights

If you are an employee of a client company, your employer is the controller of your data: requests to see, correct or delete your record go first to your employer or its payroll provider, who can act on them in the platform. Through the employee portal you can see your own record, your payslips and your leave, and update your contact and emergency details directly. Where the law of your jurisdiction gives you rights of access, correction, objection or complaint, we will assist your employer in meeting them, and you may contact us directly at the address below if you cannot reach your employer.

Security

Role-based access with multi-factor authentication required for every role that moves money or administers access; separation of duties (the person who requests a change cannot approve it); immutable committed pay runs; an append-only audit trail; encrypted storage of secrets and tokens; TLS on every connection; and routine review of access. If we become aware of a breach affecting a client's data we will notify the client without undue delay and support its notification obligations.

Changes and contact

We will post changes to this policy here and date them; material changes are notified to clients through the platform. Questions, requests and complaints: support@caycengroup.com, or Caycen Group, Castries, Saint Lucia.

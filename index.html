About

Built where it's used.

Caycen Group grew out of consulting practice in Saint Lucia — years of running payroll, compliance, and operations for real Eastern Caribbean organisations. That practice is why our products start from the statute and the workflow, not from a template built somewhere else.

  • Statute as code — the law of the jurisdiction is implemented, tested, and updated by us when it changes.
  • Governance by design — separation of duties, immutable records, audit trails, and two-factor security in every product.
  • Transparent pricing — published rates in EC$, built for organisations of every size.
  • Local, live support — from the people who built it, in the region it serves.

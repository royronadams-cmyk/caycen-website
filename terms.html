How this document is used

One set of platform terms for every client company. A direct client accepts them with Caycen and pays Caycen the fees in Schedule A. A client introduced by a Partner firm also accepts them with Caycen — for the platform — while its service relationship, fees and first-line support sit with the Partner under the Partner's own engagement letter, which attaches these Terms unchanged (Schedule B). Acceptance is electronic, in the Platform, by a user with authority to bind the company, and is recorded in the audit trail with the version accepted, the time, and the acceptor's identity (clause 11).

1. Parties And Structure

1.1 These Terms are between Caycen Group, LLC, a limited liability company organised in the State of Maryland, United States of America, operating the CaycenHR platform (“Caycen”, “we”), and the company identified in the Platform as the client company (“the Client”, “you”), the employer whose payroll is processed on the Platform.

1.2 Where the Client was introduced to the platform by an accounting, payroll or HR practice enrolled in Caycen’s Partner Programme (“the Partner”), the Partner operates the Client’s payroll on the platform and is the Client’s contracting party for services and fees. These Terms govern the platform itself and apply between Caycen and the Client regardless of the Partner’s engagement. If the Partner’s engagement letter conflicts with these Terms on any matter concerning the platform or the Client’s data, these Terms prevail.

1.3 A person accepting these Terms in the platform confirms that they hold authority to bind the Client. Caycen relies on that confirmation.

2. Definitions

2.1 “Platform” — the CaycenHR software service and its web addresses, including any Partner-branded address; “Client Data” — all data entered into or generated by the Platform for the Client, including employee personal data, pay records, filings and documents; “Committed Run” — a pay run locked in the Platform by an authorised user, after which its figures are immutable; “Statutory Outputs” — schedules, remittances, returns, slips and files generated by the Platform for a tax or social-security authority; “Authorised User” — a person given a login for the Client’s company; “Jurisdiction” — the country or territory whose law the Client’s company is configured to follow in the Platform, as shown in the Client’s organisation settings and recorded on its Certificate of Acceptance.

3. The Service

3.1 Caycen provides the Platform: statutory payroll computation for the Jurisdiction; Statutory Outputs generated from Committed Runs; payslips; an employee self-service portal; leave, severance and termination records; reports; an audit trail; and the further features of the Client’s feature plan as published.

3.2 Caycen maintains the statutory rules of each Jurisdiction in the Platform and records the authority for each rule. Rule changes take effect in the Platform from their effective date in law; where a change requires a decision by the Client (for example the classification of a worker under a sector order), the Platform asks for it and the Client provides it.

3.3 The Platform computes from the information the Client provides. It does not give legal, tax or accounting advice, and it does not file with any authority on the Client’s behalf. The Client (or its Partner on its instruction) reviews each output and submits it.

4. Accounts, Access And Security

4.1 The Client controls who its Authorised Users are and what role each holds. Roles with authority over money (approve, commit, release) require multi-factor authentication. Credentials are personal; sharing a login is a breach of these Terms.

4.2 The Client will not: use the Platform to process payroll for any employer other than itself (or, for a Partner, other than its enrolled clients); attempt to access another tenant’s data; reverse-engineer, scrape or resell the Platform; or introduce malicious code.

4.3 Caycen may suspend a login that is being used in breach of this clause or that presents a security risk, and will tell the Client’s owner promptly.

5. The Client’S Responsibilities

5.1 The Client remains the employer and remains responsible in law for paying its employees correctly and on time, for registering with and paying the tax and social-security authorities, and for the accuracy and timeliness of the information it provides.

5.2 The Client provides, and keeps current: its statutory registrations; each employee’s identifiers, pay basis, contract terms and bank details; variable inputs for each period (hours, overtime, leave taken, allowances, deductions authorised in writing); and year-to-date balances on conversion.

5.3 The Client reviews each pay run before it is approved and committed. Once committed, figures are immutable (clause 7). The Client reviews each Statutory Output before submitting it to the authority.

6. Client Data And Data Protection

6.1 Ownership. Client Data belongs to the Client. Caycen acquires no rights in it other than the licence to host, process and back it up for the purpose of providing the Platform.

6.2 Roles. For employee personal data the Client is the controller and Caycen is a processor acting on the Client’s documented instructions, which these Terms and the Platform’s functions constitute. Where a Partner operates the Client’s payroll, the Partner acts on the Client’s behalf and these roles are unchanged.

6.3 Employees. Employees access their own payslips, leave and records through the portal under the Client’s authority. The Client is responsible for informing its employees that their data is processed on the Platform, in accordance with the data-protection law of the Client’s Jurisdiction.

6.4 Security. Caycen maintains administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to payroll data: encryption in transit, access control by role, multi-factor authentication for privileged roles, an append-only audit trail, segregated tenants, and backups. Caycen will notify the Client without undue delay on becoming aware of a breach affecting Client Data, with the information needed for the Client to meet its own obligations.

6.5 Sub-processors. Caycen uses the hosting, e-mail and payment providers listed in the Data Processing Statement published in the Platform, each bound by written terms no less protective than these. Caycen gives 30 days’ notice of a change to the list; the Client may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

6.6 Confidentiality. Each party keeps the other’s confidential information confidential and uses it only for these Terms. Client Data is confidential information of the Client. Caycen may use aggregated, de-identified statistics that cannot identify the Client or any person.

6.7 Export and retention. The Client may export its complete records at any time while the account is active. On termination the Client has 90 days to export; Caycen then deletes Client Data from the live Platform within a further 30 days, retaining only what law or a legal hold requires and backup copies until they expire on their normal cycle.

7. Committed Runs, Immutability And Corrections

7.1 A Committed Run is immutable: its figures, payslips and the Statutory Outputs drawn from it cannot be edited. Corrections are made by an arrears line in a later run or by a documented reversal run, each of which is itself recorded.

7.2 A reversal requires a second authorised officer: the person who committed a run can never be the sole person who reverses it. Once a Statutory Output has been marked as filed with the authority, the run it draws on is not reversed; corrections enter the next period.

7.3 The Client acknowledges that these controls exist to protect the Client and its employees and that they cannot be disabled on request.

8. Audit Trail And Electronic Records

8.1 The Platform records every sensitive action — who, what and when — in an append-only audit trail available to the Client. The Client agrees that these records are the parties’ record of what was done on the Platform and may be relied on as such.

8.2 Each party consents to electronic records and electronic signatures for all purposes connected with these Terms, including acceptance under clause 11, approvals and commits within the Platform, and notices by e-mail to the addresses on file.

9. Fees And Payment

9.1 Direct clients. The Client pays Caycen the fees in Schedule A for its feature plan and active head-count each month, as published and amended on 30 days’ notice, plus the onboarding fee stated there. Fees are in Eastern Caribbean dollars, exclusive of VAT or similar tax where applicable, invoiced monthly in arrears and payable within 14 days.

9.2 Partner clients. Where a Partner operates the Client’s payroll, the Client pays the Partner under the Partner’s engagement letter and pays Caycen nothing for the platform. The Partner’s charge to the Client for the platform component will not exceed Caycen’s published direct price for the same plan and head-count.

9.3 Non-payment. If an undisputed invoice is more than 30 days overdue after written notice, Caycen may suspend the committing of new pay runs for the Client (or, for a Partner in arrears, for its book) until paid. Caycen will never, for non-payment, withhold access to Committed Runs, payslips, Statutory Outputs already generated, or the Client’s export.

10. Availability, Support And Changes To The Platform

10.1 Caycen will use reasonable efforts to keep the Platform available continuously, with planned maintenance notified in advance and scheduled away from month-end where practicable. A Client on the Enterprise plan has the service levels and remedies stated in its Enterprise order.

10.2 Support: a direct client contacts Caycen by the channels published in the Platform; a Partner client contacts its Partner, who contacts Caycen. Caycen may improve, add to or alter Platform features; it will not remove a statutory capability required by the Client’s Jurisdiction while the Client is active.

11. Acceptance And Changes To These Terms

11.1 These Terms are accepted in the Platform by a user who confirms their authority to bind the Client, by typing their name and choosing Accept. The Platform records the version accepted, its content hash, the time (UTC), the user’s identity and role, the network address and browser used, and whether multi-factor authentication was active, and issues a Certificate of Acceptance to the Client’s document vault and by e-mail.

11.2 Caycen may issue a new version. Material changes are notified at least 30 days before their effective date with a summary of what changed. The Platform asks a user with binding authority to accept the new version at their next sign-in on or after the effective date; until accepted, the previous version continues to apply to the Client, and routine use by other users is not interrupted.

11.3 Each published version is immutable and is retained by Caycen so that the text accepted by any Client at any time can be produced.

12. Term And Termination

12.1 These Terms begin on acceptance and continue month to month. Either party may end them on 30 days’ written notice. Caycen may end them immediately for a material breach not cured within 14 days of notice, or for the Client’s insolvency.

12.2 On termination clause 6.7 applies, and clauses 6, 7, 8, 13 and 14 survive. A Partner client whose Partner relationship ends may continue on the Platform as a direct client, with another Partner, or export and leave — the choice is the Client’s.

13. Liability

13.1 The Platform is a tool operated under the Client’s instructions. Caycen is not responsible for loss arising from inaccurate, late or incomplete information provided by the Client or its Partner, from decisions the Client makes in the Platform, from the Client’s failure to review outputs before paying or filing, or from penalties or interest levied by an authority for the Client’s late or incorrect payment or filing.

13.2 Caycen is responsible for errors in the Platform’s computation of the statutory rules it publishes for a Jurisdiction, as applied to the information provided. Where such an error causes the Client a direct loss, Caycen will correct the error and, at the Client’s choice, re-run the affected periods and re-issue outputs at no charge.

13.3 Subject to clause 13.4, each party’s total liability under or in connection with these Terms in any 12-month period is limited to the fees paid or payable for the Platform in respect of the Client in that period — for a direct client, the fees paid to Caycen; for a Partner client, the platform component attributable to the Client under the Partner’s engagement, and in any event no less than the equivalent published direct price for the Client’s plan and head-count. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, or loss of goodwill.

13.4 Nothing in these Terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, for breach of clause 6.6, or for any liability that cannot be limited by law.

14. General

14.1 These Terms are governed by the laws of the Client’s Jurisdiction, being the jurisdiction configured for the Client’s company in the Platform at the time of acceptance, and the courts of that Jurisdiction have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them. The Platform records the Jurisdiction on each Certificate of Acceptance.

14.2 Notices are given by e-mail to the Client’s owner address on file and to support@caycengroup.com. Neither party may assign these Terms without consent, except Caycen to a successor of the CaycenHR business on notice. If any clause is unenforceable the rest stand. These Terms, Schedule A (for direct clients) and the Data Processing Statement are the entire agreement on the platform; a Partner’s engagement letter governs the Partner’s services.

Schedule B — Partner-Operated Clients

Where a Partner operates the Client’s payroll on the Platform, the following apply between Caycen and the Partner for the Client’s benefit, and the Client may rely on them.

B.1 The Partner’s engagement with the Client attaches these Client Platform Terms (current version) as a schedule, unchanged, and states that they govern the Platform and the Client’s data and prevail over the engagement on those matters.

B.2 The Partner does not agree with the Client any term that contradicts these Terms, charges more for the platform component than Caycen’s published direct price for the same plan and head-count, limits the Client’s ownership of or right to export its data, or purports to disable the Platform’s immutability or four-eyes controls.

B.3 The Client’s owner accepts these Terms directly in the Platform before the Client’s first pay run is committed; the Platform does not commit a pay run for a Client that has not accepted.

B.4 On Caycen’s written request the Partner provides a copy of the Client’s engagement letter within 10 business days; Caycen treats it as confidential and uses it only to verify compliance with this Schedule.

B.5 If a Partner is in breach of this Schedule and does not cure within 30 days of notice, Caycen may suspend the Partner’s ability to onboard new clients until cured. Caycen will never, for a Partner’s breach, interrupt the Client’s payroll, its access to its records or its right to export.

B.6 The Partner records in the Platform, for each client, that an engagement letter is on file and satisfies B.1 and B.2, and certifies the same annually.
